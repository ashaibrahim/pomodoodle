      No password needed. Signing in is optional: as a guest, everything still works and is saved in this browser.

      What's saved and why

      When you sign in, Pomodoodle saves: your email address (to sign you in), your buddy and name, your timer and reminder settings, and your study progress (sessions and minutes per day, streaks, achievements).

      It's only used to sync your progress between your devices. It's never sold or shared, and there are no ads or trackers. Chat messages and rooms are never saved. Sign-in is handled by Supabase (and Google, if you choose it).

      You can delete your cloud data any time. Progress saved in this browser stays here until you clear your browser data.
